# UX Evaluation Survey: Bayangan yang Tertinggal

## Purpose

Use this compact survey after a playtest session to evaluate *Bayangan yang Tertinggal* as a Malay horror visual novel prototype. The survey focuses on the most important UX signals: narrative engagement, interaction clarity, UI readability, horror atmosphere, accessibility, and overall player satisfaction.

The survey has 3 sections and 18 total questions. Questions 1 to 14 use a 5-point Likert scale. Questions 15 to 18 are open-ended.

## Section 1: Story, Horror, And Emotional Engagement

1. The opening made a strong first impression and made me want to continue.
2. I understood the main story, setting, and character motivations.
3. The Malay horror elements felt meaningful and appropriate to the game.
4. The pacing of the story, pauses, and reveals created tension effectively.
5. The pocong felt threatening, memorable, and connected to the story.
6. I cared about what happened to the characters or village.

## Section 2: Choices, Interaction, And User Interface

7. The choices were easy to understand before selecting them.
8. The choices felt meaningful and made me think about consequences.
9. Dialogue text, character names, and menus were easy to read.
10. Buttons, choice options, and navigation screens were clear and easy to use.
11. The visual layout helped me focus on the story without feeling cluttered.
12. The game gave me enough time and control to read, choose, save, load, and adjust settings comfortably.

## Section 3: Accessibility, Polish, And Overall Feedback
